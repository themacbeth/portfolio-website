CYBERNETICS

Circuits, eyes, and the loops between them

CYBERNETICS

A collection of short video loops exploring the feedback between machine and observer — chips, optics, and connection diagrams treated as living material. Each clip is a fragment from a larger thought about systems that watch themselves.

The pieces move between the macro and the micro: an eye that reboots, a chip in montage, color flowing across nodes that have learned to anticipate each other.

The observer and the observed are circuits in the same body.

PROCESS

Each loop was generated and tuned to read as a self-contained system — short enough to feel like a heartbeat, long enough to make the feedback legible. Sequenced together they suggest a single machine remembering itself.
