Questions We Welcome

Is this a cult?

We get this question. We welcome it. A cult demands obedience to a single leader — we have no leader. A cult isolates you — we encourage letters home. We simply offer a place where the noise stops. That’s all this is.

What happens to my phone?

Your devices are lovingly stored in The Surrender Room upon Arrival. Wooden lockbox. Your name on the lid. Sealed. Most Wanderers stop missing them within 48 hours.

Who are you?

We are Reclaimers. We reclaimed our attention, our time, our presence. The Clearing is where we did it. There is no leader. There are Guides. There are Listeners. But The Clearing leads itself.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Food, shelter, clothing, community — provided. Leave your wallet. You won’t need it.

Can I contact family?

We encourage handwritten letters. We mail them for you weekly. It’s more meaningful than a text, don’t you think?

What are The Openings?

Many long-term Wanderers report seeing them. The Guides consider them a natural part of the architecture. We encourage you not to research them before Arrival. Experience is more honest than expectation.

Can I leave?

Of course. The hallways always lead to an exit. Most Wanderers find they prefer to stay. That’s a choice, not a condition.

Come home.

Not everyone hears The Quiet. The fact that you're still reading means something. We don't recruit. We recognize.
