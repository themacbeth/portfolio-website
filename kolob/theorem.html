The Theorem

One galaxy. Three glories.
A sealed core.

A cosmology of light, drawn live by the chain. Every piece a system; every system a place in the order. The galaxy is fixed in shape and shifting in breath, and a hash is enough to find your way home.

The Axis · Kolob

At the very centre, a single tight cluster of white particles: Kolob. Neither star nor throne, but the axis upon which the galaxy turns. A black-hole well wraps it, and it swallows spent worlds and casts new ones outward. It is not minted. It is what everything else is measured against, and the whole galaxy takes its name from this place. The project is KOLOB; the centre is Kolob; and the two are one.

The Triangle

Three great suns surround the axis in perfect equilateral relation, holding the inner order: Oleblish, Enish-go-on-dosh, Kai-e-vanrash. They turn together as a rigid figure, a measure of the noble Intelligences from before the foundation of light.

The Twelve

Beyond the triangle, twelve governing systems, Lindi, Zip, Vusel, Vauiste, Waine, Way-ho-ox-oan, Oansli, Kible, Shineflis, Flis, Os, Ondi, each bearing a single distinguishing Affinity: a ringed dynasty, a moon swarm, an asteroid empire, a comet storm, an embryo nursery, an eccentric chaos, a binary heart, a resonance cathedral, a frozen kuiper, a hot-jupiter forge, a mega-ring world, trojan swarms. They turn as one icosahedral solid. Together with the triangle they make the Core 15, sealed in identity, authored by those who hold them.

The Three Glories

Beyond the Core, every throne lives in one of three concentric kingdoms. Further in is rarer, denser, the light more elaborate.

Celestial  the gold inner ring, binary stars, comet swarms

Terrestrial  the richer middle, ringed giants, warmer worlds

Telestial  the cold outer rim, every throne begins here

The Veils

Between the kingdoms hang two veils, thin shimmering bands of dust. To move inward, a throne must pass through, and the passage is ceremonial: the worlds dim, dust pulls inward and swallows everything, the music drops to silence at the threshold, and the soul emerges into a richer palette. The crossing cannot be undone. It cannot be hurried.

The Trait Vocabulary

Every minted piece carries four cosmological measurements, deterministic from its hash. They are the language collectors use to speak about the worlds beyond the names.

Intelligence

The density and order of a system's particles, its inward fire. A measure of what the world remembers from before its formation.

Noble · Quickening · Veiled · Encircled · Witnessing · Risen · Wrought · Foreseen

Affinity

The orbital signature: how the worlds chord with one another. Whether they ring in whole-number resonance, fall in cascades, or hold one another bound.

Dispensation

The era of emission. KOLOB casts worlds outward in waves; a Dispensation marks which wave a piece belongs to.

Glory

Beyond the last veil there is no light, no heat, no stars. A throne that holds too long at the Telestial rim drifts outward into Outer Darkness, and the chain remembers the drift. To advance is to hold a place in the light. To wait is to slip from it.
