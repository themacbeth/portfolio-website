How should I auction the Core 15?

KOLOB is a fully on-chain generative collection launching on Art Blocks. The 129 Thrones sell at a fixed price. That part's settled. What if I auction the core 15?

What's being sold

3 Great Stars, the rarest
12 Governing Systems
  • Each of the 15 is a 1 of 1, sealed at the core
  • Holders personalise theirs after mint (star colour / archetype), on-chain
  • They're the trophy tier above the 129 Thrones
The catch

My real concern isn't price, it's depth. A big competitive auction that expects fifteen-plus serious bidders could fall flat in a thin market. So I'm weighing mechanisms by how well they hold up when turnout is low, not high.

Three ways to run it

resilience low risk

BDutch auction, buy-order

Leaning toward

One descending price. The first 3 to buy claim the Great Stars; the next 12 get the Systems. A reserve floor keeps the stars from going too cheap.

Holds up because

It clears one piece at a time as the price falls. No crowd bidding at once. Keeps the "highest commitment ascends to the core" story, and it's basically an Art Blocks-native minter.

Watch out

In a quiet market a star could get grabbed cheaply once the price drops. The reserve floor blunts that.

resilience lowest risk

DHybrid

The hedge

The 3 Great Stars run as separate ascending (English) auctions. The 12 Systems all sell at one fixed price.

Holds up because

It puts real competition only on the 3 trophies (where it'll show up even in a thin market), and the 12 can't "fail to fill." There's no bidding, they just clear.

Watch out

Two mechanisms to build and explain. Loses the single unified event.

resilience fragile

ARanked pool

Leaning against

Everyone bids into one pool. The top 3 bids claim the Great Stars; everyone below gets a System.

The appeal

Best story of the three. The highest bidders literally ascend to the three stars at the core.

Why I'm cool on it

The ranking is meaningless without 15+ bids, and it needs a custom contract to hand out the star slots by rank, which fights Art Blocks' sequential minting.
