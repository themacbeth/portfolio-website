Minting

On the Mint page, click Connect Wallet, then Mint. Confirm the transaction in your wallet. Your token arrives in under a minute. You need an Ethereum wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, WalletConnect) with a little more ETH than the mint price to cover gas.

Today the Mint page runs as a dry run: it walks you through the exact flow, but no wallet transaction is sent. The project is still in review with Art Blocks ahead of a live mint.

Exploring

Click VIEW next to your piece. The KOLOB explorer opens with your world running live. No loading, no servers. Or visit the galaxy explorer to browse the full collection. Your token is visible to any wallet, on any device, instantly.

On headphones, press A while viewing your piece: every world carries a seeded frequency identity — its own orbital ratios, quietly sounded.

Common questions
Is the artwork stored on a server or IPFS somewhere?

No. The program lives directly on Ethereum, not a file, not a cloud server. Your browser downloads and runs it from the chain every time. If Ethereum is live, your piece is live.

Do I have to advance both kingdoms, or can I stop at Terrestrial?

You can stop at Terrestrial. That is a valid final state and it protects you from the deadline. Celestial is optional. You can advance both before the deadline. Your call.

What if I sell my Throne before advancing it?

The new owner inherits the throne at whatever state it is in. They control the advancement and carry the deadline risk. If you are selling a Telestial throne close to October 2026, be transparent about it.

Is the mint live right now?

Not yet. The Mint page above runs as a dry run so you can preview the exact flow before it goes live: minting, advancing, everything. No wallet transaction is sent today. The on-chain mechanism for the "pay to advance" step is being finalized with Art Blocks ahead of the real mint.
