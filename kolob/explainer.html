The Guide

KOLOB, in plain terms

No background needed. Here's what this is and how to explore it.

What is it?

KOLOB is a generative artwork: one giant galaxy made entirely of moving pixels, with no images or files loaded — every star, planet and dust mote is drawn live by code. The galaxy is divided into three glowing rings, and scattered through it are 159 distinct worlds you can fly into and explore. Each one is built from a unique seed, so no two are alike.

✦

The three rings

Telestial

Terrestrial

Celestial

Gold core

The dense, rare centre — and the locked Core 15, the most powerful pieces of all.

✦

How to explore

Open the galaxy

Tap ENTER in the menu. You'll land in the full galaxy, slowly turning.

Look around

Drag to orbit the view. Scroll or pinch to zoom in and out. On desktop, arrow keys step between thrones.

Enter a world

Tap any glowing star to drop into its planetary system — orbits, moons, comets, asteroid belts and all.

Let it play

Tap the Cinema button for a hands-off slideshow that drifts smoothly from world to world. Go fullscreen and enjoy.

Find your way back

Press ← Back (or Esc) to return to the galaxy at any time.

✦

The on-screen buttons

  • ENHANCE — doubles the particle count for a denser, richer picture (uses more power).
  • LUMEN — brightens everything, for darker screens or a bolder look.
  • CINEMA — opens the slideshow options: play, speed, hold, and fullscreen.
  • THRONE ___ — type a number (0–143) and hit GO to jump straight to that world.
✦

Common questions

Do I need any special app?

No. It runs in any modern browser, on phone or desktop. Nothing to install.

Why do some worlds look richer than others?

Worlds belong to one of the three rings. Outer (telestial) systems start simple; advancing a world inward — through the veils — unlocks more density, more bodies, new palettes, and a signature relic.

What is the Core 15?

Fifteen sealed, one-of-a-kind pieces fixed at the gold centre: three great suns and twelve governing systems. They never change, and no ordinary world can ever match them. See them ▸

It runs slowly on my phone — what helps?

Leave ENHANCE off, and zoom into a single world rather than the whole galaxy. Fullscreen also helps.
