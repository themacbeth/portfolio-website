The Premise

What does the Milky Way look like as a work of art?

In KOLOB the galaxy is one ordered cosmos: a blazing core at the centre, three concentric kingdoms of light, two veils of dust between them, and the long migration of a single world from the core to the rim and home again. Each token is one throne in that cosmos, generated deterministically from its hash.
