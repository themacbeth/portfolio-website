THE VEIL

Laser Projection · Trippy Star Show · Immersive Ceremony

THE VEIL

A 10-minute immersive audio-visual ceremony performed in darkness. One or two laser projectors beam onto three hanging fabric veils, each veil representing a stage in the journey of the soul through the kingdoms of the heavens. A trippy star show galaxies, suns and sealed cores drifts and pulses across the fabric, audio-reactive to a pre-composed track. The light passes through each layer, splitting and diffracting as it travels deeper into the space.

Three veils. Three kingdoms. Hints of the endowment, lit only by laser.

The first veil catches the brightest field a whole galaxy spread across the weave. The second softens it to a single burning sun. The third receives only the core, a sealed point of light, barely visible.

Participants observe from either side or from behind.

TECHNICAL

One or two laser projectors beaming through three suspended fabric veils. All visuals audio-reactive to a pre-composed 10-minute devotional track. Generative starfield galaxies, suns, sealed cores drawn as continuous laser paths. 10 minutes. 3 veils.

CROWLEY THEATER · THE FINAL APPROACH

A scheduled, seated 10-minute audiovisual performance, run three times per evening with intermissions between sets to let the room re-haze, the audience cycle, and the laser cool. The piece is a secular meditation on the final veil moment of the temple ceremony, abstracted to its universal architecture, the veil threshold, the body of light beyond it, the celestial column.

KOLOB

Kolob is the on-chain companion to this installation, a generative galaxy on Art Blocks: 144 worlds, 15 sealed at the core, three kingdoms. The Veil is its embodied translation, what the chain draws as pixels, the laser and LED column draw as light.
